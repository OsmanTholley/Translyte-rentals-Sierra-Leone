# Complete Hosting & Domain Deployment Guide
## Translyte Rentals Sierra Leone (`www.translaterentalsierraleone.com`)

Congratulations! Your website for **Translyte Rentals Sierra Leone** is fully developed, responsive, interactive, and ready for deployment. Below are the easiest ways to launch your website live to the world.

## 🚀 Option 1: Hosting on Netlify (RECOMMENDED - Free, 2-Minute Setup)

Netlify is the easiest and fastest way to host static websites with **free automatic SSL (HTTPS)**.

### Step 1: Prepare Your Project Folder
Your project folder contains:
- `index.html`
- `css/styles.css`
- `js/app.js`

### Step 2: Deploy to Netlify
1. Go to [https://www.netlify.com](https://www.netlify.com) and sign up for a **Free Account**.
2. Log in to your Netlify Dashboard.
3. Click on **"Sites"** in the top menu.
4. Scroll down to the area that says **"Want to deploy a new site without Git? Drag and drop your site folder here"**.
5. Drag and drop the `c:\Translyte rentals Sierra Leone` folder directly into your browser window!
6. **Done!** Netlify will publish your site in less than 5 seconds and give you a live link (e.g. `https://translyte-rentals-sl.netlify.app`).

### Step 3: Link Your Custom Domain (`www.translaterentalsierraleone.com`)
1. In Netlify, go to **Site settings > Domain management > Add custom domain**.
2. Type in: `www.translaterentalsierraleone.com` and click **Save**.
3. Netlify will show you the DNS settings (Nameservers or A/CNAME records) to enter where you bought your domain name (e.g. Namecheap, GoDaddy, Hostinger).
4. Update your domain's DNS:
   - **Alias/A Record**: `@` → `75.2.60.5` (Netlify Load Balancer)
   - **CNAME Record**: `www` → `your-site-name.netlify.app`
5. Netlify will automatically generate a free HTTPS SSL Security Certificate!

## ⚡ Option 2: Hosting on Vercel (Free & High Speed)

1. Go to [https://vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and create a free account.
2. Drag and drop the project folder or connect your GitHub repository.
3. To attach domain: Go to **Project Settings > Domains**, enter `www.translaterentalsierraleone.com`, and add the provided CNAME/A records to your domain registrar.

---

## 🌐 Option 3: Traditional Shared Hosting / cPanel (Hostinger, Namecheap, Bluehost, GoDaddy)

If you already own cPanel hosting or Hostinger:

1. Log into your hosting account **cPanel / File Manager**.
2. Open the directory named `public_html` (or `www`).
3. Upload all project files:
   - `index.html`
   - `css/` folder (with `styles.css`)
   - `js/` folder (with `app.js`)
4. Visit `www.translaterentalsierraleone.com` in your browser. Your website will instantly load!
